# Claim 5 evaluator checklist

- Exact claim and source protocol: `claim_contract.json`, `source_audit.md`
- Full executable: `src/cdot_repro/claim5.py`
- Independent fold checker: `src/cdot_repro/claim5_checker.py`
- Method, fixed command, and deviations: `method.md`, `limitations.md`
- Raw folds, controls, selected pairs, and results: generated by formal run

Candidate verdict is `BLOCKED` until the full formal run completes.
